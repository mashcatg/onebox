Introduction

At OneBox, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our email management service.

Our Commitment: We use a zero-knowledge architecture, meaning we cannot access your email content. Your data is encrypted end-to-end.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password (encrypted)
  • Payment Information: Billing address and payment method details (processed by third-party payment processors)
  • Email Account Credentials: OAuth tokens to access your connected email accounts
  • Support Communications: Messages you send to our support team

Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: Features used, pages visited, time spent, interactions
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, error logs
  • Cookies: Session data and preferences

Email Metadata

We process email metadata (sender, recipient, subject lines, timestamps) to provide our service features like sorting and filtering. Email content remains encrypted and inaccessible to us.

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services

We use AI and machine learning to improve email sorting and filtering, but this processing happens on encrypted metadata only.
