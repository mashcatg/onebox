Security First

Your Data is 100% Secure

We take security seriously. Your emails are encrypted end-to-end and we never access your data.

  • End-to-End Encryption

    All your data is encrypted using military-grade AES-256 encryption both in transit and at rest.

  • Two-Factor Authentication

    Add an extra layer of security to your account with 2FA support via SMS or authenticator apps.

  • SOC 2 Compliance

    We're fully compliant with industry standards including GDPR, SOC 2, and CCPA regulations.

  • Zero-Knowledge Architecture

    We can't access your emails even if we wanted to. Your privacy is guaranteed by design.
