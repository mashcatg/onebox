Introduction

At OneBox, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our email management service.

By using OneBox, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our services.

Our Commitment: We use a zero-knowledge architecture, meaning we cannot access your email content. Your data is encrypted end-to-end, and your privacy is guaranteed by design.

Information We Collect

1. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and encrypted password.
  • Payment Information: Billing address and payment method details are processed securely through third-party payment processors. We do not store complete credit card information.
  • Email Account Credentials: We use OAuth tokens to securely access your connected email accounts. These tokens are encrypted and stored securely.
  • Profile Information: Any additional information you choose to add to your profile, such as profile picture or preferences.
  • Support Communications: When you contact our support team, we collect the content of your messages and any attachments you send.

2. Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you use our service, including features used, pages visited, time spent, and user interactions.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type and version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, pages viewed, referring URLs, and other diagnostic data.
  • Location Data: Approximate geographic location based on IP address to provide localized services.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to track activity and store certain information.

3. Email Metadata

We process email metadata including sender information, recipient information, subject lines, timestamps, and email size to provide our service features like sorting, filtering, and smart notifications. Important: Email content remains encrypted end-to-end and is completely inaccessible to us or our staff.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide, maintain, and improve our email management services
  • Account Management: To manage your account, authenticate users, and provide customer support
  • Transaction Processing: To process payments, send billing information, and manage subscriptions
  • Communication: To send administrative information, service updates, security alerts, and support messages
  • Personalization: To understand your preferences and provide a personalized experience
  • Analytics: To monitor and analyze usage patterns, trends, and service effectiveness
  • AI Features: To improve email sorting, filtering, and categorization using machine learning (on encrypted metadata only)
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, and security threats
  • Development: To develop new features, products, and services
  • Marketing: To send promotional communications about new features and offers (with your consent)
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share your information in the following circumstances:

1. With Your Consent

We share information when you explicitly authorize us to do so, such as when connecting third-party applications or services.

2. Service Providers

We share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf:

  • Cloud hosting providers (for infrastructure and data storage)
  • Payment processors (for billing and subscription management)
  • Analytics providers (for usage analysis and service improvement)
  • Customer support tools (to provide efficient support)
  • Email delivery services (for transactional emails)

All service providers are bound by data protection agreements and can only use your information to provide services to us.

3. Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information when required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities, including:

  • Compliance with legal obligations or court orders
  • Protection of our rights, property, or safety
  • Investigation of fraud or security issues
  • Prevention of illegal activities or violations of our terms

4. Business Transfers

In connection with any merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

Important: We never sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes. Your privacy is not for sale.

Data Security

We implement industry-leading security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

Encryption

  • AES-256 Encryption: All data is encrypted at rest using military-grade encryption
  • TLS/SSL: Data in transit is protected using Transport Layer Security
  • End-to-End Encryption: Email content is encrypted with your private keys that we cannot access

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Our zero-knowledge system ensures that only you can decrypt and read your email content. Even if our systems were compromised, your email content would remain secure and unreadable.

Infrastructure Security

  • SOC 2 Compliance: Our infrastructure meets stringent security standards
  • Regular Audits: Third-party security assessments and penetration testing
  • Secure Data Centers: Data stored in certified, monitored facilities
  • DDoS Protection: Advanced protection against distributed denial-of-service attacks

Access Controls

  • Employee Access: Strict need-to-know basis with comprehensive logging
  • Two-Factor Authentication: Optional 2FA for enhanced account security
  • Session Management: Automatic logout and secure session handling

While we implement robust security measures, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we continuously work to protect your information.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience and collect information about how you use our service.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic functionality, authentication, and security. These cannot be disabled.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings, language preferences, and customization choices.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how you use our service to improve performance and features.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements (only with your consent).

Managing Cookies

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete cookies
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies (may limit functionality)
  • Delete cookies when you close your browser

Third-Party Analytics

We use third-party analytics services to analyze service usage. These services may use cookies to collect information. You can opt-out of these analytics services through their respective opt-out mechanisms.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

General Rights

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (right to be forgotten)
  • Right to Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Object to certain types of processing of your information
  • Right to Restriction: Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing where consent was the legal basis

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, you can:

  • Email us at: privacy@onebox.com
  • Use the privacy settings in your account dashboard
  • Contact our Data Protection Officer at: dpo@onebox.com

We will respond to your request within 30 days. For security purposes, we may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe we have not adequately addressed your privacy concerns, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations:

Retention Periods

  • Active Accounts: Data retained for the duration of your active account
  • Inactive Accounts: Accounts inactive for 12 months may be flagged for deletion
  • Deleted Accounts: 30-day recovery period, then permanent deletion
  • Billing Records: Retained for 7 years for tax and accounting purposes
  • Support Communications: Retained for 3 years for quality assurance
  • Log Data: Typically retained for 90 days unless needed for security investigations

Data Deletion

When you delete your account or request data deletion:

  • Account data is anonymized or deleted within 30 days
  • Backup systems are purged within 90 days
  • Some information may be retained longer if required by law

Children's Privacy

OneBox is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at privacy@onebox.com. We will take steps to remove such information from our systems.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from children without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information as quickly as possible.
